1. 4-DOF AMB Rotor Equations & Gyroscopic Moments

Rotor Dynamics

The rover wheel hub rotor of mass $m$, transverse moment of inertia $I_t$, and polar moment of inertia $I_p$ operates under active electromagnetic levitation. As rotational velocity $\Omega = \frac{v}{R}$ scales, gyroscopic coupling links orthogonal axes $(x, \beta)$ and $(y, -\alpha)$:

$$m \ddot{x} + k_s x = k_i i_x + F_{\text{unbalance}}(t) + F_{\text{dust},x}$$
$$I_t \ddot{\alpha} + I_p \Omega \dot{\beta} - k_{\theta} \alpha = -T_{\text{coil},\alpha} + M_{\text{shear}}$$

Where $k_s = \frac{2 \mu_0 A N^2 I_0^2}{s_0^3}$ represents open-loop negative magnetic stiffness, $k_i = \frac{2 \mu_0 A N^2 I_0}{s_0^2}$ is current sensitivity, and $I_p \Omega \dot{\beta}$ is the destabilizing gyroscopic cross-coupling moment.

2. Squeeze-Film Particulate Shear & Phase Lag

Tribology & Drag

When fine lunar regolith grains (mean diameter $d_p \approx 0.5 - 20\,\mu\text{m}$) breach degraded labyrinth seals, they enter the $s_0 = 1.25\,\text{mm}$ magnetic air-gap. The particle concentration $\phi$ induces non-Newtonian granular shear and electrostatic damping:

$$F_{\text{dust}}(t) = - c_p(\phi) \dot{x} - k_p(\phi) x - \mu_{\text{coulomb}} N_{\text{contact}} \text{sgn}(\dot{x})$$
$$\Delta \theta(v, \phi) = \arctan\left(\frac{\omega c_p(\phi) + I_p \Omega / I_t}{k_s - m \omega^2}\right)$$

Particulate accumulation causes the phase angle $\theta$ between the inductive proximity displacement sensor and coil current to lag significantly toward $-90^\circ$, eroding closed-loop damping margins.

3. Dynamic Nyquist Phase-Space Mapping $(v, \theta)$

Observer Inversion

The software continuous auditor computes the open-loop transfer function matrix $\mathbf{G}(s)\mathbf{K}(s)$. The safe operating envelope in the $(v, \theta)$ coordinate space is defined by Cauchy's argument principle:

$$\text{Margin}_{\text{Phase}} = 180^\circ + \arg\left[\det\left(\mathbf{I} + \mathbf{G}(j\omega_c)\mathbf{K}(j\omega_c)\right)\right] \ge 30^\circ$$
$$\theta_{\text{boundary}}(v) = -\left[20^\circ + 0.045 \cdot v + 0.000085 \cdot v^2 + 0.4 \cdot C_d\right]$$

When operating points cross into the red hazard corridor, active damping $\zeta < 0.05$, causing unconstrained rotor orbital whirl, stator touchdown, and catastrophic wheel lockup.

4. Adaptive Pole-Placement & Real-Time Remediation

Remediation DSP

Upon detecting particulate-induced phase erosion $\Delta \theta$, the digital signal processor executes real-time updates on the dual-channel state-feedback gain matrix $\mathbf{K}$:

$$\mathbf{u}(t) = -\mathbf{K}(\phi, \Omega)\mathbf{\hat{x}}(t) - \mathbf{K}_{\text{cross}}(\Omega)\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \mathbf{\dot{x}}(t)$$
$$\mathbf{K}_{\text{lead}}(s) = K_0 \left(\frac{1 + \alpha_1 \tau s}{1 + \tau s}\right) \cdot \exp\left(j \cdot \Delta \theta_{\text{compensation}}\right)$$

This instantly shifts closed-loop system poles into the stable left half-plane ($s$-plane), recovering $>25\,\text{dB}$ damping margin and sustaining 100% non-contact levitation even under heavy regolith clogging.
